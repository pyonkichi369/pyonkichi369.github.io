## About DreamGarage Inc.

DreamGarage Inc. is a technology company maximizing development team potential through multi-agent AI orchestration.

---

## Mission

To maximize AI potential — not through a single AI assistant, but through multiple specialized AI agents working in coordination.

We're building a future where developers don't just write code — they strategize, analyze risks, and ensure quality — all alongside their AI team.

---

## Vision

We propose a new paradigm for AI collaboration.

- **AI as a Team** — Leverage AI not as a single assistant, but as a specialized team
- **Executive Decision-Making** — Recreate CEO, CTO, CFO perspectives in AI for multi-dimensional analysis
- **Democratizing Quality** — Red Team analysis and cross-reviews bring enterprise-grade quality to every project
